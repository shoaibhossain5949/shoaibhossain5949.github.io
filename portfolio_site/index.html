Where machine learning meets real-world impact

I build supervised and deep learning models in Python and TensorFlow, then use SHAP and LIME to show why a model made each prediction. That focus on transparency shaped my thesis on drug addiction in Bangladesh and my work on early stroke prediction.

Beyond research, I’ve led a peer study group, organized a university workshop on research methodology and competed in AI and programming contests.

Research

My work focuses on making machine learning understandable to the people who rely on its results.

Undergraduate thesis · Manuscript under review

Identifying the root causes of drug addiction with explainable AI

I developed an explainable framework that applies XGBoost, SHAP and LIME to uncover the socio-economic, psychosocial and environmental factors linked to drug addiction in Bangladesh. The aim is to give policymakers clear, evidence-based insight for prevention and rehabilitation programs.

Supervised by Md. Shah Jalal, Senior Lecturer, Department of CSE, Daffodil International University.
